# 🏨 Hospitality Revenue & Performance Analytics | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze hotel revenue, occupancy, pricing, booking channels, and property-level performance.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

The hotel business generates large amounts of booking and property data, but raw data alone does not provide a clear view of business performance.

The objective of this project was to transform hotel booking data into an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders:

- Monitor revenue and key hospitality KPIs
- Compare property and city performance
- Understand booking-platform performance
- Track occupancy and pricing trends
- Identify performance gaps and improvement opportunities

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Build a centralized Power BI dashboard to provide a clear view of **hotel revenue and operational performance** and support data-driven revenue and pricing decisions.

---

## 📊 What I Analyzed

The dashboard analyzes:

### Revenue & Financial Performance
- Total Revenue
- ADR (Average Daily Rate)
- RevPAR (Revenue per Available Room)
- Realization %

### Hotel Operations
- Occupancy %
- DSRN, DBRN & DURN
- Cancellation %
- Property-level performance

### Market & Customer Performance
- City-wise occupancy
- Average customer ratings
- Property-wise revenue and KPIs
- Booking-platform performance

### Trends
- Weekly revenue and occupancy trends
- ADR and RevPAR movement
- Realization % across booking platforms

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Generated **₹1.7B+ in total revenue** across the analyzed hotel portfolio.
- Overall occupancy stood at **57.87%**, with a realization rate of **70.15%**.
- **Atliq Exotica** was the highest-revenue property, generating approximately **₹320M**.
- **Delhi** recorded the highest occupancy among the analyzed cities at **60.55%**.
- Overall **RevPAR reached ₹7.35K**, showing a **27.84% improvement**.
- Booking-platform performance showed differences in **ADR and realization**, highlighting opportunities for revenue optimization.
- Property-level analysis helped identify variations in **occupancy, revenue, cancellations, and customer ratings**.
